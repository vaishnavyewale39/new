# PRAGATI AI — Website Design Direction

## Three possible directions

### 1. Field Atlas
**Very Brief Intro:** A precise, terrain-inspired intelligence interface that brings a sense of nationwide field coverage to policy analysis. It balances institutional clarity with small agricultural cues rather than decorative imagery.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Policy Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A calm, document-informed command centre where data appears as a well-organised national record. Dense information remains readable through structured paper-white surfaces and editorial hierarchy.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Monsoon Signal
**Very Brief Intro:** A fluid weather-and-market instrument panel whose blue, green, and amber status signals clarify shifting supply conditions at a glance. The mood is composed, analytical, and distinctly Indian without becoming ornamental.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Monsoon Signal

### Design Movement
**Civic data modernism**: a contemporary public-service dashboard inspired by cartographic instruments, statistical yearbooks, and Indian monsoon mapping. It is intentionally more rigorous than a consumer app and more legible than a traditional BI tool.

### Core Principles

1. Lead with a decisive answer, then make the supporting evidence easy to inspect.
2. Use information density deliberately: compact analytics modules sit within ample, disciplined whitespace.
3. Let blue frame the application, green signal stable/favourable conditions, amber signal attention, and red remain exceptional.
4. Use charts, state signals, and data labels as the primary visual language; agricultural illustration is reserved for the brand mark and identity moments.

### Color Philosophy
The application begins in paper-white and faint blue-grey so data feels official and trustworthy rather than promotional. **Pragati Blue** anchors navigation and system intelligence. Leaf green communicates normal or stable price conditions, harvest amber conveys forecast attention, and a restrained red is reserved only for risk. The intended emotional progression is clarity, confidence, then timely action.

### Layout Paradigm
The desktop experience is a **pinned navigation rail plus reporting canvas**: a narrow intelligence rail establishes system location while the canvas arranges blocks on an uneven editorial rhythm. A wide market picture and price graph establish context, then a narrow right-hand action rail surfaces watchlists and alerts. Tablets collapse the rail; mobile becomes a stacked brief with a bottom action dock.

### Signature Elements

1. Fine blue grid rules and a subtle contour-line backdrop that echoes seasonal maps without obscuring data.
2. A three-state market signal device: green stable, amber watch, red intervention.
3. A compact timeline marker that visibly divides observed prices from the AI forecast horizon.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should reduce uncertainty. Search anticipates valid commodities; filters are explicit; tabs alter only the relevant analytical view; hover states expose a small amount of contextual data; and confidence labels accompany every model-derived output. Controls use brief, crisp motion and never imitate entertainment interfaces.

### Animation
On first load, key metrics count up over roughly 650ms, insight cards lift and fade in with 45ms stagger, and price paths reveal from left to right. Forecast regions fade in only after historical data is visible. Active controls transition with a 160ms ease-out and buttons compress to 0.97 on press. Animations respect reduced-motion preferences, and no chart continuously animates after first render.

### Typography System
**Manrope** is the analytical workhorse for controls, tables, and body copy because its numerals remain clear at small sizes. **Lora** provides restrained editorial emphasis for page headlines and system statements. Headline hierarchy is Lora 600 at 28–36px; section headlines use Manrope 700 at 14–18px; operational data uses Manrope 500/700 with tabular numerals; labels use uppercase Manrope 700 with expanded tracking.

### Brand Essence
**Pragati AI is the decision intelligence layer for public authorities managing India’s essential commodity price stability.** It is **measured, responsive, and authoritative**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be direct and evidence-led; CTAs should name the analytical action; microcopy should specify data basis and confidence instead of using generic encouragement.

> “Price pressure is forming. Review the evidence before it becomes a spike.”

> “Compare retail, wholesale, rainfall, and forecast signals.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is an abstract **rising crop-row signal**: three forward-leaning strokes housed in a rounded, open square, suggesting both cultivated rows and an upward market trajectory. The PRAGATI wordmark is set in a custom-spaced Manrope bold rather than a default logo treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Pragati Blue — #123E78.** A deep, civic-blue signal designed to remain stable beside changing market-status colors.

## Style Decisions

- Desktop navigation always carries the custom-spaced **PRAGATI AI** wordmark and crop-row signal mark as part of a clear national command-centre rail.
- **Pragati Blue #123E78** frames intelligence; green indicates stable or favourable conditions; amber denotes attention; red is reserved for exceptional price pressure or intervention review.
- Headlines and actions name the commodity, decision context and evidence basis. The dashboard leads with the current pressure signal and then supports it through observed price, rainfall context and forecast confidence.

## Blue-Green Current Replacement

The revised direction replaces the vertical rail with a **horizontal blue-to-green operational bar**, creating a broader analysis canvas and a different navigational rhythm. The surface system uses water-blue for intelligence, river-green for status and action, and a pale aqua canvas for data clarity. The dashboard composition shifts to a hero-led briefing, a compact metric ribbon, and alternating wide/narrow analysis blocks, with subtle current-like motion in signal cards, markers and chart surfaces.

## 3D Tide Room

The command centre now reads as a layered spatial instrument panel rather than a flat dashboard. The selected market signal sits in the foreground, metric tiles occupy staggered depth planes, and map, watchlist and chart panels lift independently as the user moves a desktop pointer. Motion is intentionally shallow and is disabled for touch and reduced-motion contexts so evidence remains legible.

## Cinematic Signal Motion

The spatial system now includes an atmospheric particle field, slow orbital paths, travelling light beams, periodic data scans and foreground-card light passes. The intention is to make live price intelligence feel active and dimensional. Animations are isolated to composited properties, deliberately paused for reduced-motion preferences, and pared back on touch devices.

## SIH Spatial Lattice — chosen replacement direction

### Design Movement
**Civic spatial computing**: an SIH-ready intelligence environment that pairs public-service credibility with the visual language of augmented-reality control rooms. It is not a gaming interface; illumination, floating depth planes, and digital coordinates clarify information hierarchy.

### Core Principles
1. Establish one crisp blue-green command surface rather than many competing effects.
2. Use glass-like depth planes only to group a decision, not as decoration.
3. Make data motion informative: reveal a trend, show a changed value, or guide the next action.
4. Retain quiet reading areas so the system remains credible in a judged technical demonstration.

### Color Philosophy
**Deep Signal Blue** grounds the page in technological confidence, while **Quantum Teal** illuminates real-time intelligence and **Civic Mint** supports legible data surfaces. A narrow use of solar amber calls attention to exceptional risk. The overall impression is a controlled digital environment rather than a neon spectacle.

### Layout Paradigm
The interface becomes a **mission-surface composition**: a slim top command bar, an asymmetric hero briefing split between narrative and a floating live-signal prism, then horizontal data bands that alternate a large analytical instrument with a compact contextual module. Mobile preserves the hierarchy as sequential cards with the live signal always first.

### Signature Elements
1. A faint perspective lattice and coordinate labels behind major modules.
2. Floating translucent data prisms with a precise blue edge-light.
3. Self-drawing paths for price and data-route indicators, used only during view entry.

### Interaction Philosophy
Navigation and chart controls use short light sweeps, a 2–4px lift, and deliberate active states. The live model signal breathes only when data is available. Chart panels reveal an interface reticle on hover; no continuous movement competes with the numbers.

### Animation
Section entries rise 10px with a 280ms custom ease-out. Headlines reveal as a clipped upward line; numeric cards count in once; data traces use a one-time stroke-dash reveal. Hover actions use 160–220ms transform and opacity transitions. Horizontal panels scroll with snap alignment on small screens; vertical sections retain native smooth scrolling. All ambient motion and self-drawing effects are disabled under reduced motion.

### Typography System
**Space Grotesk** handles technical headings and data labels; **DM Sans** provides high-legibility body copy. Headings use Space Grotesk 600 with tight tracking, while statistics use tabular figures and a higher contrast weight. Avoid ornamental display effects that impair readout speed.

### Brand Essence
**PRAGATI AI turns nationwide commodity signals into an immersive, explainable decision surface for SIH demonstration and public-service teams.** It is **precise, forward-looking, and composed**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are operational and concise. CTAs name the next analytical task rather than promoting the product.

> “Signal detected. Read the market before it shifts.”

> “Trace the price movement. Validate the context.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The existing rising crop-row signal becomes a hovering **agri-orbit glyph**, framed by a thin coordinate reticle and used with an exact, horizontally spaced PRAGATI AI wordmark.

### Signature Brand Color
**Quantum Teal — #22D3C5.**

### Style Decisions
- Quantum Teal is reserved for live model status, active analytical controls, and primary data traces; secondary decoration remains blue-green.
- Each analytics panel shows one immediate decision readout and one clearly visible data instrument.
- CTAs name the analytical action, such as “Review evidence” and “Trace signals,” never generic product commands.
